    I’m a PhD student in the Department of Linguistics at Stony Brook University, advised by Dr. Thomas Graf. I am primarily interested in computational linguistics and syntax. Currently, I'm working on using ideas from formal language theory to interrogate claims about the (non-)shared properties of syntax and phonology.
